## Contract changes

SQLite schema 5 → 6 adds immutable delivery claim/release, retention pin and tombstone records; the v1.0 wire schemas remain unchanged. Retention is an explicit local operation, not a background queue. A stale, missing or cross-workspace report source is rejected at admission, before it can reserve evidence. A successful delivered report releases its claim when the operation finishes. An uncertain container retains the workspace lock until the exact owned container is removed and its removal is journaled; an empty inventory after an ambiguous create is not proof of absence. Hard evidence ENOSPC is a FAULT with no false run record and no implicit retry. These contracts matter to EP-05 delivery and EP-07 recovery/installation.
